About Fantasy Biscuit

One biscuit. Zero distractions.

Fantasy Biscuit started as a simple question: what if a website had exactly one job? No product lineup, no newsletter pop-up — just a biscuit, golden and proud, sitting quietly on the page.

It turns out a biscuit can do a little more than sit there. Click it, and it'll fetch yesterday's MLB box scores for you — because a good biscuit understands that people have places to be and box scores to check.

We don't sell anything here. We don't track much of anything either. Fantasy Biscuit is just a small, well-made corner of the internet, built with the same care as a good biscuit: simple ingredients, done properly.
